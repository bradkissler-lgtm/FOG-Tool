Picture a single ladder. At the bottom, financing is something you hand to someone else. At the top, you own all of it — the approval, the spread, the customer who comes back, the dealer who stops shopping around. The FOG is the distance from where you stand to the top of that ladder, counted in dollars.

The OEMs near the top didn't add another bank. They built the captive economics into the sale, one rung at a time. The further down you are, the more of it is still sitting there — which is why the widest gap belongs to the program doing the least, and the narrowest to the one already most of the way up.

A financing program isn't a cost center or a profit center. It's a decision about who keeps the customer.
